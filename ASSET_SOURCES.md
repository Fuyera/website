## laofu canvas

`public/images/laofu-canvas-scenes.png` is the approved English scene illustration from the laofu canvas 0.3.0 launch materials, reused without image editing. It illustrates supported screen-annotation scenarios; it is a marketing illustration, not a captured application session. Product behaviour is cross-checked against the current product materials and [official support page](https://laofuai.com/products/laofu-canvas/support).

## Professional system diagrams

The capability maps and interactive demonstrations are authored in HTML/CSS/SVG for this website. They illustrate documented processing concepts: mission corrections, operator-confirmed fields and a separate annotation layer. All events, transcript text and underlying document content are synthetic examples. These are labelled illustrations, not captured product interfaces or live AI services. They contain no real mission, patient, call, customer or deployment data.
